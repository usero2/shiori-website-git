# How to Highlight Text and Take Notes in an Android EPUB Reader

There is something deeply satisfying about stumbling upon a sentence that resonates with you, marking it in a gentle color, and noting down the thought that just sparked in your mind. **Shiori** is designed to make this rhythm effortless: highlight any passage in a single tap, attach your personal reflections, and revisit every insight in a beautifully organized space — the **Memory** tab.

---

## 1. Eight Harmonious Colors for Every Thought

Select any text and tap **Highlight**, then choose from eight gentle shades: Yellow, Green, Blue, Pink, Orange, Purple, Teal, or Red. The highlight applies immediately without breaking your reading flow and stays permanently with your book — perfect for color-coding memorable quotes, key takeaways, character notes, or new vocabulary.

## 2. Give Every Highlight a Voice with Notes

A highlight captures what the author said; a note captures what *you* felt. Choose **“Highlight with a note…”** to jot down a reflection, a quick translation, or a personal reminder. You can edit your notes or switch colors whenever inspiration strikes, keeping your private thoughts neatly tied to the text.

## 3. The Memory Tab: Your Personal Commonplace Book

Every highlight and note you create flows naturally into the **Memory** tab. Filter by **Highlight** or **Note**, search across your entire library, or tap any card to instantly return to that exact paragraph in the book. You can even export your favorite quotes into elegant cards to share with friends. It’s more than a list — it’s your personal treasury of knowledge and inspiration.

---

## Highlight & Take Notes in Five Simple Steps

### Step 1: Select Text and Tap "Highlight"
Long-press any word to start a selection, drag the handles smoothly to encompass the phrase or passage, then tap **Highlight** on the floating menu.

### Step 2: Pick Your Favorite Color
Choose from eight curated colors. The highlight applies instantly without annoying confirmation popups, keeping your reading completely uninterrupted.

### Step 3: Saved Instantly — Add a Note
The passage is now highlighted in your chosen color. A gentle shortcut lets you **add a note** right away — or you can simply continue reading and add one later.

### Step 4: Write Down Your Reflections
In the **Highlight & Note** editor, write your thoughts, insights, or translations, and feel free to change the color anytime. Everything saves automatically the moment you close the editor.

### Step 5: Rediscover Everything in the Memory Tab
Open the **Memory** tab to browse all your highlights and notes across every book. Filter by type, search for keywords, tap a card to jump straight to that exact paragraph, or share it as an elegant visual card.

> **💡 Pro Tip:** Create your own gentle color system — like warm yellow for quotes, cool blue for vocabulary, and calming green for actionable ideas. When you filter by color in Memory, reviewing insights feels effortless.
