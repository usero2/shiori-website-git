# Read Along with Karaoke Highlighting in Shiori EPUB Reader

Listening to text-to-speech (TTS) while visually following the words on the screen—often called bimodal reading—is one of the most effective ways to boost reading comprehension, stay focused, and acquire new languages. When your eyes and ears receive the same information simultaneously, your brain processes vocabulary and sentence structure much faster than through listening or reading alone.

However, many reading apps treat audio narration and visual reading as completely separate experiences. Some play sound without showing where you are in the chapter, while others apply harsh, distracting highlight bars that clash with dark or AMOLED themes.

Shiori EPUB Reader bridges this gap with built-in, synchronized karaoke-style highlighting. As the voice reads aloud, Shiori tracks the text at multiple levels—paragraph, chunk/sentence, and individual word—allowing you to customize highlight colors and opacity to match your reading style. This walkthrough shows how to set up and customize karaoke highlighting on your Android device.

## Why synchronized karaoke highlighting matters

Whether you are studying a foreign language, reading a complex academic text, or dealing with digital reading fatigue, synchronized visual highlighting provides distinct advantages:

* **Language learning & pronunciation**: Hear native pronunciation while tracking exact spelling and word boundaries in real time.
* **Focus & ADHD support**: The moving highlight serves as a visual anchor, keeping your eyes from wandering off the page during dense paragraphs.
* **Comfortable contrast**: With custom color presets and transparency controls, highlights remain easy on the eyes in both bright day themes and pitch-black dark modes.

## What you need

* Shiori installed on your Android device (tested on v2.3.4)
* Any EPUB book in your library (this guide uses *The Prince* by Niccolò Machiavelli)
* Android Text-to-Speech engine enabled (such as Google Speech Services or your device's system default)

## Step 1: Open TTS Listening Settings

You can configure your highlight preferences directly from the app settings before starting playback:

1. From the bookshelf, tap the **Settings** icon in the top toolbar.
2. Select **TTS Listening** to open the read-aloud configuration screen.
3. Scroll down to the highlighting section. You will see separate controls for **Paragraph highlight**, **Chunk highlight**, **Word highlight**, and **Aloud word (translation)**.

![TTS Listening settings screen in Shiori showing options for Paragraph highlight, Chunk highlight, Word highlight, and translation highlight levels](01-tts-highlight-levels-settings.jpg)

## Step 2: Customize Word Highlight Color and Opacity

The word highlight is the active "karaoke" cursor that moves from word to word as the voice speaks.

1. Tap **Word highlight** in the TTS Listening menu.
2. The color picker dialog appears with 10 distinct color presets (including purple, yellow, orange, red, blue, and green) alongside a theme default option.
3. Use the **Opacity** slider below the color swatches to adjust transparency (e.g., 50–55% provides clean visibility without obscuring the underlying text).
4. Check the live preview in the **Sample of highlighted text** box, then tap **OK** to save your choice.

![Word highlight color picker dialog showing 10 color swatches, opacity slider set to 55 percent, and a sample highlighted text preview](02-word-highlight-color-picker-dialog.jpg)

## Step 3: Configure Paragraph and Chunk Highlighting

In addition to word-level tracking, Shiori can highlight the entire active paragraph or phrase chunk to provide visual context:

1. Tap **Paragraph highlight** in the settings list.
2. Choose a soft accent color and a subtle opacity level (such as 25–30%) so the paragraph block frames the reading area without overpowering the active word.
3. Tap **OK** to confirm.

![Paragraph highlight color picker dialog in Shiori showing color selection options and opacity slider set to 28 percent](03-paragraph-highlight-color-picker.jpg)

## Step 4: Start Reading Aloud and Watch Visual Tracking in Action

Now that your preferred colors and opacity levels are set, open a book to see real-time karaoke tracking in action:

1. Open any book from your bookshelf.
2. Tap the **Play / Pause** button on the bottom control bar (or tap the headphone icon in the top toolbar).
3. As the TTS engine begins speaking, Shiori highlights the active paragraph with your chosen container color and moves the purple highlight cursor smoothly across every spoken word.

![Reading page in The Prince showing real-time karaoke word highlight on the active spoken word with paragraph bounding box](04-karaoke-word-highlight-in-action.jpg)

## Step 5: Continuous Synchronized Playback Across Chapters

As you listen and read along, Shiori automatically scrolls the view to keep the active sentence centered on your screen. You can control playback using the bottom toolbar:

* **Play / Pause**: Start or stop narration at any time.
* **Skip Previous / Next**: Jump between paragraphs without losing sync.
* **Speed & Pitch**: Adjust playback speed to match your comfortable reading pace.
* **Continuous Reading**: Shiori seamlessly reads across chapter boundaries, automatically turning pages and scrolling without requiring manual interaction.

![Reader view with full audio playback controls showing synchronized karaoke highlight on the word collectively during continuous reading](05-synchronized-reading-karaoke-with-controls.jpg)

## Helpful Tips

* **Floating Controls**: Turn on **Floating Controls** in TTS Listening settings if you want to keep reading controls accessible while switching between apps or taking notes.
* **Per-Language Voices**: Shiori lets you assign specific TTS engines and voice variants for different languages under **TTS Languages**, ensuring accurate pronunciation for multilingual libraries.
* **Read Selected Text**: If you only want to hear a single difficult sentence rather than playing the entire chapter, long-press the text in the reader and select **Speak** from the context menu.
