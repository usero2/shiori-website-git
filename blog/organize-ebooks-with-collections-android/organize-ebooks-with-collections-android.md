# A Place for Every Book: Organize Your Library with Collections

A growing book collection is one of life's greatest pleasures — until your shelf becomes so vast that finding the right book feels like searching for a needle in a haystack. **Collections** in Shiori are designed to bring harmony back to your library. Rather than forcing your books into rigid folders, Collections give you the flexibility to group titles however your mind naturally categorizes them: by genre, favorite authors, current study topics, cozy weekend reads, or reading mood. Give each shelf a distinct color, tuck your books inside, and watch your personal library transform into an inviting, beautifully curated haven.

---

## 1. What You Can Do with Collections

- **Create unlimited custom shelves**, each styled with its own distinct accent color.
- **Add or remove books in a few gentle taps** — a single book can effortlessly belong to multiple collections at once (e.g., both "Sci-Fi Classics" and "Favorites").
- **Rename, recolor, and reorder shelves** anytime your reading tastes evolve.
- **Delete collections worry-free** — removing a shelf never deletes the actual book files from your library.
- **Filter your entire bookshelf** by reading status — *Reading*, *Unread*, *Want to Read*, or *Finished*.

## 2. Two Delightful Ways to Explore Your Library

Whether you want to dive into a specific topic or see what's on your immediate reading radar, Shiori gives you two instant paths. Tap any collection shelf to view only its carefully curated titles, or tap a reading status tile at the top of the Collections tab to focus your whole bookshelf on what you are actively reading, books waiting to be started, or finished gems you might want to revisit.

---

## Step-by-Step Guide (6 Steps)

### Step 1: Discover the Collections Tab
Open the **Collections** tab from the bottom navigation. At the top, you'll find quick reading-status tiles. Right below, **My Collections** displays all the personalized shelves you've created, each with its vibrant color dot and book count.

### Step 2: Create a New Collection in Seconds
Tap **Create new** (or the **+** icon beside My Collections). Type a memorable name for your shelf, choose a color that reflects its spirit, and tap **Save**. Your new shelf is ready immediately.

### Step 3: Add Books to Your Shelves
On your bookshelf, simply long-press any book and select **Collection**. Check off all the shelves you'd like the book to appear in, then tap **Save**. To remove a book later, just revisit this menu and uncheck the shelf.

### Step 4: Browse Inside a Collection
Tap any collection to view its contents. Each book also wears subtle, colored badge tags showing every collection it belongs to, giving you instant context at a glance.

### Step 5: Rename, Reorder, or Delete Shelves
Long-press any collection to open its management menu. Here you can **Rename** the shelf, update its color, shift its position left or right to reorder your list, or **Delete** it. Rest assured, deleting a collection never affects your book files.

### Step 6: Filter by Reading Status
From the Collections tab, tap any status tile — **Reading, Unread, Want to Read,** or **Finished** — to instantly filter your entire bookshelf. When you're done, tap the **×** on the active filter chip to return to your full library.

> **💡 Pro Tip:** Color-code by genre or mood — calm green for science fiction, deep blue for history, warm rose for all-time favorites. Because books can live on multiple shelves simultaneously, you never have to choose just one place for a beloved title.
