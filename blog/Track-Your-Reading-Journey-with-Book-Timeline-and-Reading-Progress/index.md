# Track Your Reading Journey with Book Timeline & Reading Progress

Finishing a book is rewarding—but seeing **how you got there** can be just as motivating.

Most ebook readers show your current page or completion percentage. Once you close the book, that journey is often forgotten.

Shiori EPUB Reader introduces **Book Timeline**, a feature that lets you look back at your reading progress and understand your reading habits over time.

## Your Reading Is More Than a Percentage

A progress bar tells you where you are.

A timeline tells the story of your reading.

Instead of only showing "65% completed," Book Timeline helps you see when you read, how your progress changed over time, and how close you are to finishing.

Whether you read every day or only on weekends, your reading history becomes part of your personal library.

## Track Every Book Individually

Each book keeps its own reading insights, making it easy to revisit your reading journey.

You can view:

* 📈 Reading progress
* 📅 Reading timeline
* ⏱️ Time spent reading
* 📚 Individual book statistics

Every book has its own history, helping you understand your reading habits instead of just displaying a percentage.

## Stay Motivated to Finish More Books

Long books can feel overwhelming.

Seeing your progress grow over days or weeks makes it easier to stay motivated and build a consistent reading habit.

Book Timeline is especially useful for:

* Novel readers
* Students
* Researchers
* Language learners
* Anyone working through long books

Small reading sessions quickly become meaningful progress.

## A Better Way to Remember Your Reading

Books are more than files—they're experiences.

Looking back at your reading timeline helps you remember when you started, how far you've come, and the effort you've invested in each book.

Instead of simply reaching the last page, you can appreciate the entire journey.
