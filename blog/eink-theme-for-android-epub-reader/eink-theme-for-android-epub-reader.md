# How to Use the E-Ink Theme for Paper-Like EPUB Reading on Android

If you read for hours on your phone or on an e-paper device, a bright, colorful, animated interface can quickly become tiring. The **E-Ink theme** in Shiori strips reading back to its essentials: crisp black text on a clean white page, with nothing moving to distract you.

It was designed for e-ink and e-paper Android devices such as Onyx Boox, but it works beautifully on any phone or tablet when you want the calmest, most paper-like reading experience possible.

## What the E-Ink Theme Does

Choosing E-Ink changes more than just the page color. It reshapes the whole reading experience for high-contrast, low-distraction displays:

* **Pure black on pure white** — maximum contrast for the sharpest, most legible text.
* **Animations turned off** — page changes are instant, so e-paper panels never smear or "ghost" during motion.
* **Flat, clean design** — soft shadows and gradients are replaced with clean outlines that e-ink screens render perfectly.
* **Applied across the whole app** — not just the reader, but your bookshelf, collections, and reading statistics too.

## Why Read in E-Ink Mode

High contrast and a still, quiet interface are easier on the eyes during long reading sessions. On real e-paper hardware, turning off animations also avoids the ghosting and flicker that motion causes, and a mostly-white, static screen is gentle on battery.

Even on a regular LCD or OLED phone, many readers prefer E-Ink mode simply because it feels like paper — no glow, no color, no motion, just the words.

## Turn On the E-Ink Theme (4 Steps)

### 1. Open Reading Settings & Choose "E-Ink"

Go to **Settings → Reading** (or open the reading settings while a book is open). Under **Theme**, tap **E-Ink**. The change applies instantly — no restart required.

### 2. Read in Crisp Black on White

Open any book and enjoy pure black text on a clean white page. Page turns are instant, so text stays sharp and never ghosts — exactly what e-paper displays are made for.

### 3. The Whole App Goes E-Ink

E-Ink isn't limited to the reader. Your bookshelf switches to a flat, high-contrast layout with clean outlines instead of shadows — easy to see and comfortable to browse on any e-paper screen.

### 4. Even Your Reading Stats Stay Readable

Your reading calendar and statistics render in the same clean, monochrome style — so tracking your progress looks great on e-ink too.

> **Tip:** On real e-paper devices, keep animations off and brightness low. E-Ink mode already disables motion for you, so pages stay crisp with no ghosting.

## Read the Way Paper Feels

Download Shiori EPUB Reader and switch on the E-Ink theme for calm, high-contrast, paper-like reading on any Android or e-paper device.
