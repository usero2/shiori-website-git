# Why Tracking Your Reading Statistics Can Help You Read More Books

Have you ever finished a great book and wondered how much time you've actually spent reading this year?

Many readers set goals like reading 20, 50, or even 100 books a year. But without tracking your progress, it's easy to lose motivation or underestimate how much you've accomplished.

That's where reading statistics can make a real difference.

## Why Reading Statistics Matter

Tracking your reading isn't about turning books into a competition. It's about understanding your reading habits and celebrating your progress.

Simple statistics can answer questions like:

* How many books have I finished?
* How much time have I spent reading?
* How many days in a row have I read?
* Which books took me the longest to finish?
* How often do I listen with text-to-speech?

Seeing your progress visually can make reading more rewarding and help you stay consistent.

## Build Better Reading Habits

One of the easiest ways to build a habit is to measure it.

Even reading for just 15–20 minutes every day can lead to dozens of finished books each year. Watching your reading streak grow gives you a small sense of achievement that encourages you to keep going.

Instead of focusing only on finishing books, you can celebrate every reading session.

## Share Your Reading Journey

Reading doesn't have to be a solitary hobby.

Sharing your reading statistics with friends or on social media can inspire others to read more and help you stay accountable to your goals.

Whether you've finished your first book of the month or reached a 100-day reading streak, every milestone is worth celebrating.

## More Than Just an EPUB Reader

Modern reading apps do much more than open EPUB files.

They can help you:

* Track reading statistics
* Monitor reading progress
* Read aloud with Text-to-Speech (TTS)
* Translate books into multiple languages
* Highlight important passages
* Add notes and bookmarks
* Sync your library across devices

These features help transform reading into a more enjoyable and productive experience.

## Discover Your Reading Patterns

After tracking your reading for a few weeks, you may notice interesting patterns.

For example:

* You read longer on weekends.
* Audiobooks or TTS help you finish more books.
* Short reading sessions add up to hours each month.
* Certain genres keep your attention longer than others.

These insights can help you create a reading routine that works for you.

## Start Tracking Today

Every page you read is progress.

Whether your goal is to build a daily reading habit, finish more books, or simply understand your reading journey, tracking your reading statistics is a simple way to stay motivated.

Small reading sessions become big achievements over time.

Your next reading milestone starts with the first page.

Whether you've finished your first book, reached a 30-day reading streak, or simply want to show how much you've read this month, sharing your progress is a great way to stay motivated and inspire others.

Your shared reading card can highlight information such as:

* 📚 Books you've finished

Sharing your reading statistics isn't about competing with others—it's about celebrating your personal reading journey.
