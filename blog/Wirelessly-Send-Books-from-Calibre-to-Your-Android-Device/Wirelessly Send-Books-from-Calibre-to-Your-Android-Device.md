# Wirelessly Send Books from Calibre to Your Android Device

Managing an ebook library with **Calibre** is one of the best ways to organize your books. But transferring them to your Android device doesn't have to involve USB cables or cloud storage.

With **Shiori EPUB Reader**, you can connect directly to **Calibre's Wireless Device** feature and send books over your local Wi-Fi network.

It's fast, simple, and keeps your reading workflow uninterrupted.

---

## Why Use Wireless Transfer?

Many readers still transfer books by:

* Connecting a USB cable
* Copying EPUB files manually
* Uploading books to cloud storage

While these methods work, they add extra steps every time you want to read a new book.

With a wireless connection, you can send books from Calibre to your Android device in just a few clicks.

---

## How It Works

The workflow is simple:

1. Open **Calibre** on your computer.
2. Enable the **Wireless Device** connection.
3. Connect **Shiori EPUB Reader** on the same Wi-Fi network.
4. Send your books directly from Calibre.
5. Start reading immediately.

No USB cable.
No manual file copying.
No cloud upload required.
